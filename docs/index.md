# Paddock

**Map the real attack paths from your internet-facing surface to your crown
jewels** — plus SCA, threat intel, IoT/OT discovery and an adaptive risk
model, in one self-hosted platform.

Paddock is a **defensive** attack-surface management platform for teams
defending their own infrastructure. It discovers assets from passive sources,
politely probes only what you have authorized, correlates findings against
public vulnerability and threat feeds, and shows the result as a graph of the
paths an attacker would actually take.

!!! warning "Authorized use only"
    Point Paddock only at assets you own or are explicitly, documentedly
    authorized to assess. Every outbound request is scope-checked and
    audit-logged. There is no exploit framework, no credential attack and no
    evasion — see the hard rules in the project README.
