# Paddock Contributor License Agreements

Paddock is open source under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**
(AGPL-3.0). The project is also offered under **commercial licenses** (see
[docs/EDITIONS.md](docs/EDITIONS.md)) — that is what funds full-time work on
the open-source code. To be able to offer both, the project needs the right to
distribute every contribution under both, so contributions are accepted under
one of the two agreements below. They are modelled on the Apache Software
Foundation's Individual and Corporate CLAs (ICLA/CCLA), adapted for AGPL +
commercial dual licensing.

**What you keep:** you keep the copyright in your contribution. This is a
license grant, not an assignment, and you remain free to use your own
contribution for anything else.

**What we promise in return:** every contribution accepted under this CLA will
also remain available under the AGPL-3.0 (or a later version of the GNU AGPL,
or another OSI-approved license) for as long as the project distributes it.
The commercial right exists so the project can be sustained, not so it can be
closed.

> This document is a template prepared for the project and is **not legal
> advice**. The maintainers should have it reviewed by counsel in the
> jurisdiction of the legal entity that will hold the agreements ("the
> Project Steward") before relying on it.

**How to sign:** on your first pull request the CLA Assistant bot will ask you
to comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Signatures are recorded (GitHub username, PR, timestamp) in the
`cla-signatures` branch of this repository. Corporate signatories should
open an issue titled "Corporate CLA: <company>" so a maintainer can record the
Schedule A list of authorized contributors.
